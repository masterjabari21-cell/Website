Side A — About Me

A little context.

I'm Jabari, and I'm a computer science student at Clemson University (B.S. anticipated December 2027) from Sumter, South Carolina. I was originally at an HBCU called Benedict College. This summer, I got the opportunity to intern as a software engineer at SYNK, building real-time and 3D features for an AI-powered virtual event platform with Next.js, WebRTC, and Three.js.

Before that I dug through large-scale transaction data as a Business Analyst intern at Capital One and built ML defect-prediction models for composite manufacturing as a NASA EPSCoR student researcher. I'm a two-time first-place hackathon winner, an AWS Student Builder campus leader, a Clemson Forge fellow, and a ColorStack & SEO EDGE member. I like problems where real-time systems, data, and AI meet — and I love raving, nature, and music, as you could probably tell.
